---
  Privacy Policy — Dance Card

  Last updated: May 20, 2026

  ---
  Dance Card ("the app") is designed with your privacy as a default, not an afterthought. This
  policy explains what data the app uses and where it lives.

  ---
  What the app stores

  Dance Card stores the following information locally on your device:

  - Session package records (quantities, dates, prices)
  - Recurring class schedules (days, times, instructor names)
  - Dancer names and session balances you enter
  - Studio event details
  - Budget estimates and notes

  All of this information is saved using your device's local storage. It never leaves your 
  device. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no servers, and no cloud sync.

  ---
  What the app does not do
  
  - No data collection. The app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information
  with the developer or any third party.
  - No analytics. The app does not include any analytics, crash reporting, or usage tracking
  tools.
  - No advertising. The app contains no ads and no advertising networks.
  - No third-party services. The app does not connect to the internet for any purpose.

  ---
  Push notifications
  
  Dance Card can send local reminders for upcoming classes and studio events. These notifications
   are scheduled entirely on your device — no data is sent to any server to generate them. You
  can turn notifications off at any time in Settings → Notifications, or in your device's system
  Settings → Notifications → Dance Card.
  
  ---
  Your data, your control
  
  Because all data is stored locally on your device, you are in complete control:

  - To delete your data: Open the app → Settings → Reset All Data.
  - To delete everything: Delete the app from your device. All stored data is removed
  permanently.

  The developer has no access to your data and cannot recover it on your behalf.

  ---
  Children
  
  The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone. It has no data collection
  mechanism whatsoever, making it suitable for all ages.

  ---
  Changes to this policy
  
  If this policy changes in a meaningful way (for example, if a future version adds cloud sync),
  the updated policy will be published at this same URL before the update is released.
